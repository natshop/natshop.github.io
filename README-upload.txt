2. Copy ALL files into your natshop.github.io folder (GitHub Desktop) and replace when asked.
3. Commit and push. Wait 1-2 minutes.
4. Open natshop.github.io/smart-center.html
5. Edit smart-center.html: replace YOUR_USERNAME in the TG line with your Telegram username.
