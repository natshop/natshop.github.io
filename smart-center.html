Help

How does the free trial work?

All courses marked FREE TRIAL open fully, with no payment. Later, full access will cost the price shown. Courses marked Coming soon are being built.

How do I install it as an app?

Android: tap Install at the top, or the browser menu then Install app. iPhone: tap Share then Add to Home Screen. After the first visit, the courses also work offline.

Are my progress and notes saved?

Yes, on your own device and browser. If you clear your browser data, they are removed.
